Sample Case Studies

Examples of how IVRTech combines technology, strategy, and execution to improve business outcomes.

Fashion Brand

Client Type: Mid-sized direct-to-consumer fashion company.

Challenge: Paid traffic was increasing but checkout conversion remained inconsistent.

What IVRTech Delivered: Conversion-focused landing pages, Shopify UX refinement, and automation for campaign offers.

Outcome: Stronger campaign consistency, better purchase flow, and improved sales predictability across launches.

Retail E-commerce Seller

Client Type: Multi-channel retail seller running Shopee and TikTok Shop.

Challenge: Team struggled with manual campaign planning and slow reaction to performance changes.

What IVRTech Delivered: Store optimization plan, campaign workflow framework, and dashboard for visibility and actions.

Outcome: Faster operational response, cleaner campaign execution, and improved conversion quality.

Wholesale Business

Client Type: Distributor managing reseller and dealer accounts.

Challenge: Bulk orders and dealer pricing were handled manually across messages and spreadsheets.

What IVRTech Delivered: Wholesale portal with account tiers, structured ordering, and flash-sale module for inventory movement.

Outcome: Smoother B2B order handling, clearer pricing governance, and stronger partner satisfaction.
